Company

Accountability infrastructure for the age of agents

HyperMindQ builds the systems that let organisations give AI agents real work while keeping people answerable for every action.

What we do

HyperMindQ builds Praetor, the accountability platform for AI agents: discovering the AI acting in an organisation’s name, encoding what each agent may do, enforcing it before execution, detecting what goes wrong and explaining every decision.

The name comes from Rome, where the praetor was the magistrate who held authority and administered the law. Praetor gives that role to software: it holds every agent to the authority it has been given.

Our design draws on research into brain-inspired, event-driven computing and neuro-symbolic architectures, and on practical frameworks for delegating decisions to AI. How it was built →

Commitments

How we work

People hold authority

The platform enforces decisions people make. It never widens an agent’s mandate on its own.

Collect only what accountability needs

Actions, rules, outcomes and the people involved. Not the content of a client’s business beyond that.

Claims we can evidence

We do not publish client names, logos or results without permission, or performance figures we cannot support.

Explanations from the rule applied

Records state the rule that was actually applied, not an after-the-fact rationalisation.
